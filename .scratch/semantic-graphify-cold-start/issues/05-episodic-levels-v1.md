## Question

What levels of context does cold-start care about?

## Answer

Already in the north star. Agents need layered orientation, not one blob of “relevant docs”:

- Prefer **current status / open / dangerous** and **decisions** in the brief.
- **Summaries** of threads/sessions as support.
- **Raw** only as evidence links, not as the main cold-start payload.

Missing a higher level → say weak/missing, don’t invent a status from chat spam.
